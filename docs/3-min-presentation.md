Slide 1: Problem (0:00 – 0:40)
"Germany runs on 629,000 registered clubs, 628,992 to be exact, a record high. But here is the problem: volunteer engagement is down to 36.7 percent, from 40 percent in 2014.

Right now, club boards spend their evenings trying to find local sponsors using gut feeling, old phone books, and generic mass letters. They face three major pain points: no time after work, no specialized sales know-how, and zero continuity when board members change.

My name is Timon, club board member and educator for club management, and together with Nabil, an AI and IT security engineer, we built a solution."

Slide 2: Solution (0:40 – 1:15)
"Meet Sponsorenkompass: sponsor data and CRM for local clubs, all in one place. It takes just three minutes to find the right local sponsors and generate pitch-ready proposals.

Here’s how it works: first, we scan every local business around the club. Second, our AI matches them with concrete sponsorship packages where every single reason is backed by a real source. And third, it generates customized outreach letters and phone scripts ready to send.

The goal? Making your club the one everyone asks: ‘How did you get all these sponsors?’"

Slide 3: Product & Validation (1:15 – 1:55)
"We built this during the hackathon for the exact people who chase sponsors every season.

As you can see in the live demo: every match explains why the business fits—for instance, pointing out local branch locations or existing sports sponsorships. The pitch letter is drafted instantly with market-based price suggestions, and the built-in pipeline keeps all contacts organized.

When we showed this to Adam, Head of Sports at a club in Konstanz, his reaction was immediate: ‘I’d pay someone 25 percent of every sponsorship they bring in.’"

Slide 4: Business Model & Go-To-Market (1:55 – 2:35)
"Our pricing is simple: 169 Euros per year for full access, which pays for itself with a single sponsor. The analysis and three matches stay free.

Out of 629,000 clubs in Germany, our own estimate is that about one in ten actively seeks sponsors. That is about 63,000 clubs and a 10.6 million euro SAM. Ten percent of that SAM in three years is our SOM: about 6,300 clubs and 1.1 million euros ARR.

We start with a Football First strategy through our direct network of club boards.

Long-term, our roadmap expands beyond subscriptions: first into broader club management software, and eventually into capturing transaction commissions directly on deals closed through our platform."
